        Straight answers

        Straight answers

        Is this just scraps?

        It’s trim, and we say so on purpose. It’s the finely textured meat, fat, and connective tissue that comes off the bone saw while whole animals are being broken down for people, collected fresh the same day. Most premium food is made from the same material. The difference is that we’ll name it.

        Why does trim cost this much?

        Because collecting it fresh, keeping it cold, and portioning it by hand is the expensive part, not the cut. You’re paying for the handling. Current pricing is on the listing.

        “Supplemental only” — so it doesn’t really do anything?

        It makes food get eaten. It’s a topper, a mixer, and a way to get the regular meal finished. It is not a balanced standalone diet. Your dog’s complete nutrition comes from their regular food.

        “Composition varies by batch” sounds like you don’t know what’s in it.

        We know what’s in it: meat, fat, and connective tissue from beef, pork, and chicken trim, and nothing else added. What varies is the ratio, because it depends on what was broken down that day.

        Raw and frozen is a hassle.

        Keep it frozen, thaw what you need, and treat it like the raw meat it is. Portion a container into a tray, freeze it in cubes, and pull one at a time.

        Is “restaurant-quality sourcing” just a marketing word?

        It’s a claim about where the material came from. This is trim off animals that were being butchered for people, caught during that process. The meat is USDA-inspected and comes straight from human-food distributors.

        Why not just buy a complete commercial food?

        Keep feeding it. This isn’t a replacement for it. It sits on top of the regular meals, not in place of them.
